A JOURNEY INTO THE DEEP

Explore the Ocean Zones

Dive from the sunlit surface into the mysterious depths of the ocean. Each zone has its own conditions and fascinating marine life.

ZONE 01 0–200 m

Sunlight Zone

Where sunlight reaches

This is the brightest ocean layer. Sunlight supports photosynthesis, allowing tiny phytoplankton to grow and support marine food webs.

Life you may find: Dolphins, sea turtles, and many reef fish.

ZONE 02 200–1,000 m

Twilight Zone

A world of fading light

Sunlight becomes very faint here. Plants generally cannot grow through photosynthesis, and many animals make daily journeys toward the surface to feed.

Life you may find: Lanternfish and some squid.

ZONE 03 1,000–4,000 m

Midnight Zone

A realm of darkness

No sunlight reaches this zone. The water is cold, the pressure is high, and some animals produce their own light through bioluminescence.

Life you may find: Anglerfish and gulper eels.

ZONE 04 4,000–6,000 m

Abyssal Zone

The vast deep-sea floor

This zone includes large areas of the deep ocean floor. It is extremely cold, completely dark, and home to animals adapted to scarce food.

Life you may find: Giant isopods and tripod fish.

ZONE 05 6,000 m and deeper

Hadal Zone

The deepest ocean trenches

Found mainly in deep ocean trenches, this is the deepest ocean zone. Organisms here must survive immense pressure and total darkness.

Life you may find: Snailfish and tiny deep-sea crustaceans.

Note: Depth boundaries are approximate and may vary slightly between scientific classifications.
